# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest earned and the total amount based on the principal amount, interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest (SI) is calculated using the following formula:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

- P = Principal amount
- R = Rate of interest per year
- T = Time period in years

## Example

Suppose:

- Principal = ₹10,000
- Rate = 5% per year
- Time = 2 years

Then:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

SI = ₹1,000

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹11,000

## Features

- Calculates simple interest
- Calculates the total amount
- Easy to understand
- Simple and beginner-friendly

## How It Works

The user provides:

1. Principal amount
2. Annual interest rate
3. Time period in years

The calculator then calculates the simple interest using the formula:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100
